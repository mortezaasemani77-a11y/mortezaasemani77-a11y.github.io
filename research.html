Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of linguistics, language education, literacy, children's learning, inclusive education, and emerging technologies.

My current areas of interest include:

  • Linguistics & Language Education — Research on language structure, vocabulary development, word formation, literacy, and approaches to language and vocabulary education, with a particular focus on Persian.
  • Vocabulary & Literacy — Exploring systematic approaches to vocabulary development and the role of lexical knowledge in reading and language learning.
  • Inclusive Education & Educational Media — Investigating the relationship between language, learning, accessibility, and educational media, particularly in relation to children with special educational needs.
  • Information Literacy & AI in Education — Exploring information literacy and the educational use of artificial intelligence, with an emphasis on personalized and accessible learning environments for children.
  • Children's Language & Media — Studying the relationship between language, storytelling, visual media, and children's learning.

Current Research Projects

1. Persian Word Families and Vocabulary Education

A research project examining the definition and identification of word families in Persian and developing a systematic framework for vocabulary education based on morphological and semantic relationships.

2. AI-Supported Personalized Information Literacy

A research project exploring the design of personalized information-literacy education supported by artificial intelligence for children with hearing-related educational needs.

3. Language, Power and Narrative in Cinema

An interdisciplinary research line examining language, silence, omission, power, and visual narrative in cinema, with particular attention to the films of Abbas Kiarostami.
