About

Morteza Asemani · Linguistics Researcher · Educator · Filmmaker

About Me

I am a linguistics researcher, educator, and filmmaker with more than three decades of professional experience across education, higher education, cinema, television, and educational media.

I hold a PhD in Linguistics and have worked as a teacher and university lecturer, while also pursuing a long-term career in filmmaking and children's media. These experiences have shaped an interdisciplinary approach to language, learning, visual storytelling, and education.

My work brings together language, education, children's media, inclusive learning, and emerging technologies.

My research interests include linguistics, language and literacy, vocabulary development, language education, inclusive education, information literacy, and the use of artificial intelligence in learning.

Education & Academic Experience

I hold a PhD in Linguistics and have extensive experience in teaching, educational administration, teacher training, and university education.

My academic interests have developed through the intersection of linguistic research, educational practice, and experience working directly with learners and teachers.

Film & Children's Media

Alongside my academic and educational work, I have pursued filmmaking for more than three decades, working in cinema, television, and educational media.

My professional work includes directing and producing fiction, documentary, and educational films. My films have been selected for national and international festivals and have received awards and recognitions.

A significant part of my experience has involved developing educational media for children and students with special educational needs.

Current Interests

I am currently interested in interdisciplinary research and professional collaboration at the intersection of language, education, children's media, inclusive learning, information literacy, and artificial intelligence.

I am particularly interested in projects that connect research with practical educational and creative applications.
