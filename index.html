About Me

I am a linguistics researcher, educator, and filmmaker with more than 30 years of experience in education, cinema, television, and educational media.

My professional interests lie at the intersection of language, education, children's media, inclusive education, and emerging technologies.

Research

My research interests include linguistics, language and literacy, children's language, inclusive education, educational media, and AI-supported learning.

Education & Teaching

I hold a PhD in Linguistics and have extensive experience as a teacher, educational administrator, university lecturer, and educator of students with special educational needs.

Film & Children's Media

I have worked for more than three decades in filmmaking and television, directing and producing fiction, documentary, and educational works.

My work has been presented at national and international film festivals, and I have produced more than 50 educational films for students with special educational needs.
