Selected Projects

My current projects bring together linguistics, language education, inclusive learning, children's media, and interdisciplinary approaches to education and visual storytelling.

Persian Word Families and Vocabulary Education
Linguistics · Vocabulary Education · Persian Language

A research project focused on redefining and identifying word families in Persian and developing a systematic framework for vocabulary education based on morphological and semantic relationships.

AI-Supported Personalized Information Literacy
Information Literacy · AI in Education · Inclusive Learning

A research project exploring a personalized educational framework for information literacy supported by artificial intelligence, with a focus on accessible learning environments for children with hearing-related educational needs.

Language, Power and Narrative in Cinema
Linguistics · Film Studies · Visual Narrative

An interdisciplinary research line examining language, silence, omission, repetition, power, and visual narrative in cinema, with particular attention to the films of Abbas Kiarostami.

Children's Educational Media and Inclusive Learning
Children's Media · Educational Film · Special Education

An ongoing professional and creative line of work connecting filmmaking, educational media, children's learning, and accessibility. The work draws on extensive experience in producing educational films for students with special educational needs.

Interdisciplinary Approach

Across these projects, I am interested in connecting research with practical educational and creative applications.

My work explores how language, visual storytelling, educational media, and emerging technologies can contribute to more effective, accessible, and meaningful learning experiences.
