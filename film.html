Film and Media

Alongside my academic and educational work, I have worked in filmmaking, television, and educational media for more than three decades.

My experience includes directing and producing fiction films, documentaries, television programs, and educational media, with a particular interest in children, education, and socially engaged storytelling.

Selected Film Work

Shoes — Director, 2016

A feature-length fiction film selected for numerous national and international festivals and recipient of multiple awards.

Red-Blue — Director, 2019

A short fiction film recognized at children's and educational film festivals, including the Isfahan International Festival of Children and Young Adults and the Roshd International Film Festival.

The Story Still Remains — Documentary

A feature-length documentary presented at international documentary film festivals.

Television & Educational Media

My television work includes directing and producing programs for Iranian national television, including documentary, cultural, educational, and live programs.

  • Production and direction for Channel One programs, including educational and documentary series.
  • Production and direction for Channel Three programs.
  • Production and direction of live television programs for the News Network.
  • Production and direction of the 100-episode series Mina, developed for teaching Persian to non-Persian speakers in cooperation with Saadi Foundation.

Children's and Inclusive Educational Media

A significant part of my professional experience has focused on educational media for children and students with special educational needs.

I established and developed two educational media studios, Eshareh and Avina, and have produced more than 50 educational films for students with special educational needs.

This experience has shaped my interest in the relationship between language, visual storytelling, accessibility, children's learning, and inclusive education.

Festivals & Recognition

My films have been selected and recognized at national and international film festivals. My festival experience includes events in the United States, United Kingdom, Italy, India, Taiwan, Kashmir, Bali, Poland, and other locations.

Selected works have received awards and recognitions in children's, educational, and national film festivals.

Film, Language & Education

My filmmaking practice and academic research are closely connected. I am particularly interested in how film and visual storytelling can create educational experiences, communicate meaning beyond spoken language, and support children's learning.

This interdisciplinary perspective informs both my creative work and my research in linguistics, education, children's media, and inclusive learning.
